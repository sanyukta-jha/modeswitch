# ModeSwitch

*what you need tonight.*

ModeSwitch is a YouTube feature concept designed to solve one specific problem: 
YouTube knows what you've watched. It doesn't explicitly know what you're here for.

## The Problem

YouTube's recommendation engine is built on history. It knows your past — 
what you watched last Tuesday, which creators you return to, 
what content performed well with users like you.

What it doesn't know is your present.

Whether your brain is fried after work or you have 10 minutes to kill. 
Whether you want to laugh or learn or just have something on in the background. 
Whether tonight is a wind-down night or a motivation night.

The result: a homepage that is simultaneously highly personalized 
and completely context-blind.

---

## The Concept

ModeSwitch adds one missing signal to YouTube's homepage: 
**session intent.**

Not a mood selector. Not a profile switcher. An intent layer — 
a lightweight system that captures what you're trying to get 
from this session before the algorithm starts guessing.

Built and researched across:

- Jobs-to-be-done research across user conversations
- Behavioral funnel analysis across 4 session stages
- Competitive analysis of short-form platform discovery models
- Full metrics framework — north star, leading indicators, guardrails
- Tradeoffs and product boundaries
- Phased roadmap starting from the smallest testable intervention

---

## The Feature

**Suggested Mode** — YouTube reads time, device, and recent session 
behavior and makes a call before you have to. 
"Want something easy tonight?" One tap. No configuration required.

**Your Modes** — Permanent, personal, porous. Create named modes, 
seed with channels or topics, and YouTube learns from how you 
actually watch inside each one.

**Quick Modes** — Temporary missions. Japan trip. Interview prep. 
Fully isolated so they never reshape your permanent recommendations.

---

## The Hypothesis

> Intent-aware recommendation systems outperform history-aware 
> recommendation systems at the moment a viewing session begins.

The success of ModeSwitch is not whether the feature ships. 
It's whether this hypothesis survives contact with real user behavior.
